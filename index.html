FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What founders ask before they book a call. Short answers first, detail where it matters.

What does an AI automation agency actually do?

Genworq builds and runs AI agents and automation systems inside the tools you already use. In practice that means inbound enquiries answered within minutes, appointments booked without back-and-forth, your CRM, inbox, calendar and invoicing kept in sync, and your business found in Google and cited by AI answer engines such as ChatGPT and Perplexity.

Which businesses is Genworq built for?

Founders and operators of service businesses that already generate demand but leak it: solar installers, e-commerce brands, household-installation firms, clinics, real estate, legal, home services, accounting, construction, gyms and automotive. If enquiries arrive and someone on your team has to chase them, it can be automated.

How long does it take to get an automation system live?

A typical first system goes live in about six weeks: a one-week audit, two weeks of blueprint and sign-off, then three weeks of build and live testing before handover. Smaller pieces, such as a single lead-response agent, ship faster. Optimisation continues after handover for as long as you keep us on.

Do I need to replace my CRM or other tools?

No. We connect the tools you already use, such as your CRM, inbox, calendar, invoicing and ad platforms, into one system. Replacing a tool only comes up when that tool is the bottleneck, and we say so during the audit, before anything is built.

Who owns the system after handover?

You do. The agents, workflows and data live in your own accounts, and we train your team to run them. You can keep Genworq on to monitor and tune the system, or take it over entirely. Nothing is locked behind us.

What are AEO and GEO, and why do they matter?

AEO, answer engine optimisation, makes your content the direct answer in featured snippets and AI answer boxes. GEO, generative engine optimisation, gets your business cited inside answers from ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google AI Overviews. Together with classic SEO they decide whether customers find you when they ask an AI instead of typing into a search box.

How much does it cost?

Every engagement starts with a free 30-minute strategy call, followed by a fixed-scope quote after the audit, so you know the price before we build. We don't publish a rate card because a single lead-response agent and a full operations rebuild are very different jobs.
